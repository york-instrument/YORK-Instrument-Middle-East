Gas analysis engineering calculators

Free, single-file calculators for moisture, dew point and oxygen measurement. Each one works offline in any browser. Maintained by YORK Instrument – Middle East; method notes and worked examples are on the online tools page.

  • Dew point calculator — Converts pressure dew point to atmospheric dew point, ppmV, mg/m³ and lb/MMscf, with a pipe condensation check online version
  • Natural gas water content — Water dew point at line pressure ↔ lb/MMscf, mg/Sm³ and ppmV, checked against a pipeline specification online version
  • SF₆ moisture calculator — SF₆ dew point to ppmV and mg/kg, dew point at filling pressure, IEC 60376 / IEC 60480 checks online version
  • ISO 8573-1 air class checker — ISO 8573-1 water class of compressed air from a dew point reading at any pressure online version
  • Nitrogen purge calculator — Time, nitrogen volume and cost to purge a vessel or pipeline to a target O₂ (flow-through, pressure-cycle, pipeline) online version
  • Hydrogen purity checker (ISO 14687) — Checks water, oxygen and other impurities against ISO 14687 Grade D for fuel-cell hydrogen online version
  • Sample line response time — Transport time and T95 response of a gas sample line from tubing size, length, flow and pressure online version
  • Moisture and oxygen unit converter — ppmV, ppmW, mg/m³, mg/Sm³, lb/MMscf and dew point; %, ppm, ppb and partial pressure for oxygen online version
  • Analyzer selector — Five questions to pick a moisture, oxygen or CO₂ analyzer type for an application online version
